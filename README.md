## Nexatel Customer Churn Analysis

**Project Status: In Progress**

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer data from Nexatel to understand customer churn patterns, identify factors associated with customer attrition, and derive meaningful business insights that can help improve customer retention.
The project is being developed in multiple phases, beginning with Data Cleaning and Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), followed by deeper analysis, insights, and recommendations.

---

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Understand the overall customer churn rate.
* Analyze customer behavior and characteristics associated with churn.
* Identify factors that may be related to customer attrition.
* Analyze the relationship between unresolved complaints and churn.
* Examine churn patterns across different states.
* Analyze retention campaign outcomes.
* Understand how churn changes over time.
* Identify customer segments that may require greater retention efforts.
* Translate analytical findings into actionable business insights.

---

## Phase 2 — Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) 

Exploratory analysis was performed to understand customer behavior and identify patterns related to churn.

1. Overall Churn Analysis: Calculated the overall churn rate to understand the proportion of customers who have left Nexatel.
2. Active vs Churned Customers: Compared active and churned customers to understand the current composition of the customer base.
3. Churn & Unresolved Complaints: Analyzed the relationship between unresolved customer complaints and churn rate to investigate whether customers with unresolved issues show different churn patterns.
4. Geographic Churn Analysis: Analyzed customer churn across states to identify geographic patterns and regions with higher numbers of churned customers.
5. Retention Campaign Analysis: Examined customer outcomes associated with retention campaigns to understand patterns in customer retention and churn.
6. Monthly Churn Analysis: Calculated monthly churn rates to analyze how customer churn changes over time.
